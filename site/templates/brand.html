Usage

  • Do keep generous padding around the mark — at least half the mark's height.
  • Do use the white version on any background darker than Cream.
  • Don't recolor the primary mark or apply drop-shadows / outer glows.
  • Don't crop, distort, or rotate the mark.
  • Don't imply partnership or endorsement without written confirmation from Divinci AI.
