# Versioned AI releases. Excellence, every time.

Divinci is the safety, security and governance layer for custom language models and agents. Evaluate before you ship, release with human sign-off and instant rollback, and hand regulators verifiable evidence for the EU AI Act, GDPR, HIPAA and NIST AI RMF.

## Enterprise AI, governed end to end

Unlock robust release management and rigorous quality assurance for large language models. Our platform equips enterprises with advanced tools to streamline deployment, enforce compliance, and ensure consistent, high-quality AI performance at scale.

### Key Features

- **Pre-release safety evals** - Regression, safety and red-team suites gate every release
- **Audit-ready evidence** - Approval records and vIndex receipts for regulators and auditors
- **Instant rollback** - Restore any previous release in seconds

### Core Capabilities

1. **Intelligent release orchestration** - Automate and coordinate complex deployment pipelines with precision. Ensure every model release is validated, documented, and seamlessly transitioned to production, minimizing risk and maximizing operational efficiency.

2. **Proactive model oversight** - Monitor model performance and compliance in real time. Leverage automated checks and comprehensive analytics to detect issues early, uphold regulatory standards, and maintain optimal model integrity.

3. **Tailored performance analytics** - Establish and track custom evaluation metrics aligned with your business objectives. Analyze accuracy, bias, and other critical factors to inform strategic decisions throughout the model lifecycle.

4. **Integrated team collaboration** - Streamline cross-functional reviews with structured workflows. Assign responsibilities, collect feedback, and document approvals to ensure every release meets organizational and regulatory expectations.
