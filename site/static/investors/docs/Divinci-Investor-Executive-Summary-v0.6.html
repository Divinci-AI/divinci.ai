Divinci AI — removing a person from an AI model, with evidence a third party can check

What we sell. Companies that run their own AI models may be asked to erase a person under GDPR Article 17. Deleting the source data does not delete what the model learned. Divinci finds what a model has learned about a person, removes it, re-measures the rest of the model against thresholds set in advance, and issues a signed receipt an auditor can verify without trusting us. The platform around it has been in production since 2023.

Proved — built and measured today

  • The receipt is live. Signed receipts are served in production and checked by an open-source verifier that needs no Divinci account.
  • We measure the damage, including our own. Our August test caught untouched topics degrading more than the erased one (−0.058 vs −0.026). On 24 September a wider check of an edit made with our shipping toolchain found it also damaged 8 of 100 other facts, although the file change itself was confined to one row. We publish both.
  • Edits are reversible. Removing a patch restores the original model byte for byte.
  • A person-level test with rules set in advance. On one small open model (Google Gemma 4 E2B), five made-up people were fine-tuned in, then removed by rebuilding the fine-tune without them from the verified public base, and compared with six models that never saw them. Every informative recovery attack fails (9 of 9); the base is verified and the result re-derives.
  • Exact deletion of facts our tool inserts. The deleted model hashes identical to the original.

Not yet proved — what the gate must show

  • The usability check. "Still a usable model" is undecided (instruction-following 0.931× the base; the interval 0.862–0.983 straddles the 0.90 bar). By the pre-set rule the recovery result is not erasure evidence until this passes, so the person-level test has not passed.
  • Removal cheaper than a rebuild. Gradient-unlearning methods have not met their bars; edits do not remove a fine-tuned-in person.
  • A second, larger model. Nothing yet beyond one small model.
  • Removal, not suppression, for facts learned in pretraining. Our strongest deletion of the "Paris" fact came back after two small retraining steps. We make no erasure claim for pretraining data.
  • Edits that are narrow in behaviour. The file changes are narrow and reversible; the effect on the model's other answers is not yet narrow enough.
  • Demand and cost. No buyer interviews, design partners or partner-sourced prospects yet. No measured cost per erasure.

The 30-day gate

Each line passes or fails on criteria written down before it runs.

  1. Person-level erasure. All five checks pass on Gemma, then on a mid-sized model from a second family (target: DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash), against control models that never saw the person. Any failed or undecided check is a fail.
  2. Independent validation. An external German AI engineering firm designs and runs an adversarial test. A German technology and data-protection law firm writes an opinion on GDPR Article 17 and AI Act Article 53(1)(c) based on it. About €30k, capped.
  3. Willingness to pay. 15–20 interviews with privacy, DPO and AI leads: who holds the budget, what triggers the purchase, and whether $60–150k a year per model is credible. Three design partners signed, each with a written scope, success criteria and a paid next step if the criteria are met.
  4. Partner activity, measured. We have seven signed software-consultancy partnerships and no introductions or pilots from them yet. The gate counts introductions, meetings and qualified prospects per partner.
  5. Cost per erasure. Today we know only compute time: 3.4–6.7 GPU-hours on one cloud GPU per test run. The gate measures the full cost of one erasure on the second model.
  6. Reachable market. A bottom-up count of organisations running open-weight models with EU exposure. We do not use top-down market reports as a plan.

Why Divinci, if Hirundo adds receipts

Hirundo (Tel Aviv, $8M seed) removes learned data from the same model families and is ahead of us on funding, certification and distribution. Its results are its own tests. Our receipt lets a buyer's auditor check the before-and-after measurements independently, and we publish failures. Hirundo could add signed receipts in weeks. That is the race the gate is timed against.
