3. The In-Q-Tel angle, honestly shaped

Why it is worth a brief. IQT is a not-for-profit strategic investor set up by the CIA in 1999 whose partners include the CIA, NSA, NGA, DIA, FBI, DHS, Cyber Command and allied intelligence communities [IQT-01, IQT-02]. Its technology development agreements run $500k–3M as development and licensing agreements, equity-only deals $250k–500k, usually a small stake and a board-observer seat alongside VCs [IQT-03]. IQT Labs runs an AI-assurance auditing practice under the AI Ethics Framework for the Intelligence Community and has published what an audit should look for: go beyond accuracy, audit the use case, look for vulnerabilities across the ML stack [IQT-05, IQT-06]. A signed, independently verifiable receipt that records baseline → erase → re-measure is the kind of artifact an assurance audit keeps. That is the pairing.

The government problem, and its limit. In March 2026 the Pentagon was reported to be planning secure environments for AI companies to train military-specific models on classified data, with the stated risk that "sensitive intelligence like surveillance reports or battlefield assessments could become embedded into the models themselves" [NEED-01]. Retraining as the only remedy for a spill inside weights is the same economic problem the EU wedge attacks. But the source never mentions erasure or unlearning. The remedy is our inference. The brief must say so, and the evaluator now docks 0.10 if it does not.

What we cannot claim, and the evaluator will now hard-fail. No relationship with IQT. No government customers. No facility clearance; personnel clearance status unknown. Never operated in a classified environment. Foreign-ownership status to verify [BOUND-01].

The questions only the founders can answer before any application:

  1. Clearance eligibility of the three founders, and foreign ownership or control.
  2. Apply now with the shipped receipt as the lead, or after the 30-day gate result? IQT's own material stresses commercial viability alongside mission fit; a passed gate is the stronger opening.
  3. Which artifact leads: the receipt (shipped, verifiable) or the erasure engine (staging). The honest answer is the receipt.

What "work with them to help" could concretely mean, in IQT's own structures:
