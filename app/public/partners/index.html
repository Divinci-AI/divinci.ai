Questions partners ask

Can my clients see each other's content?

Not unless you deliberately wire them together. Each client gets their own workspace, and a workspace's content is only reachable from that workspace — you can invite a client's staff into theirs without giving them any visibility into the others. Routing a query from one release to another is a separate feature that is off by default, fail-closed, and limited to an explicit allowlist of targets.

Can I use the demo pipeline on my own prospects?

Yes — that is what it is for, and it is Apache-2.0 so there is nothing to ask us about first. Point it at a prospect's public site and it produces a working, citation-grounded assistant under their brand that you can open in a first meeting. It never contacts anyone on its own: outreach is behind a hard human gate, and the crawl asks before it crawls. The source is here.

Whose brand does the client see?

Yours, or theirs. The embedded assistant carries the logo, colours and copy you set per workspace — nothing on the widget says Divinci unless you want it to.

Do I have to resell your subscription?

No. Plans are monthly usage-credit packs, so how you package that into your own retainer or project fee is entirely up to you. If you are bringing several clients at once, talk to us about terms.

Can each client use their own model provider?

Yes — bring-your-own-key is set per workspace, so a client who insists their data goes only to their own OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, xAI, Together, Moonshot or Cloudflare account can have exactly that, and their model usage bills to them rather than to you.

Can I automate provisioning?

Yes. There's a REST API, typed SDK packages and a divinci CLI, so creating a workspace, loading content and publishing a release can be steps in your own pipeline rather than a click-path.

How do I know a change didn't make it worse?

Score the assistant against a rated question set, A/B test two configurations, or run them head-to-head in Arena — before the change reaches the client's visitors. These are on every plan, not an upgrade.

What happens when an engagement ends?

Remove your team from the workspace and hand it over, or delete it. Unless you connected it to another release yourself, the client's content was only ever reachable from their own workspace, so there is nothing to untangle.

What if a client's security review asks for SSO?

Single sign-on — Okta OIDC or SAML — is configured per workspace and sits on the Enterprise plan, alongside a dedicated support engineer. Custom domains are Enterprise-only and in early access; ask us where that stands for your timeline.

How long does the first one take?

Ingesting a client's website or document set takes minutes, not weeks — the same funnels anyone else uses. The engagement time goes into tuning and evaluating it, which is the part your clients are paying you for.
