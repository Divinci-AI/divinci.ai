    Questions people ask

    Is it really free?

    Yes — building and trying your AI is free, no credit card. If you grow past the free tier (lots of visitors chatting), there's simple flat pricing.

    What file types can I upload?

    PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets (XLSX and CSV), slide decks, e-books, plain text and Markdown — plus audio and video, which get transcribed first.

    How big can my file be?

    Large documents are fine — a few hundred pages processes in a couple of minutes. Very large files take longer, and you'll see progress while it builds.

    Will it make things up?

    It answers from your document's actual content and shows the passage it used. If your file doesn't cover something, it says so instead of guessing.

    Who can see my documents?

    Only you, unless you choose to share. We don't sell data, and you can delete your files and your AI whenever you like. Privacy policy.

    Can I add more files later?

    Yes. Add files anytime from your dashboard and your AI folds them into the same knowledge base, answering across all of them at once.

    Can I use it outside Divinci?

    Yes — share a link, embed it on any website in one line, or connect it to ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, or Gemini through the Divinci MCP.

    What if my document is in another language?

    That's fine. Your AI reads the document in its original language and answers in whichever language the reader asks in.

    I have a website, not a file.

    Use the website funnel instead — paste your link and it reads your whole site. You can add files to the same AI afterwards.
